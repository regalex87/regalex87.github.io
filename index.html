PRIVATE, OWNER-OPERATED UTILITY

Marketing Knowledge Base

A personal system for organizing, checking and maintaining a private collection of marketing knowledge in Google Drive and a private GitHub repository.

This application is intended for its owner, not for public accounts or third-party customers.

What the application does

Organize sources

Identify new text documents in designated Google Drive knowledge folders and compare them against the processing record.

Maintain knowledge

Support review, synthesis, version control and backup of authorized knowledge-base documents. Automated updates are enabled only after testing and owner approval.

Check integrity

Compare private GitHub snapshots with Google Drive documents, detect differences and retain records of changes.

Google Drive access

The application asks its owner to authorize Google Drive access so it can manage selected knowledge-base documents. The current Google Drive OAuth scope grants broad file permissions; the owner's automation is designed to operate on designated folders only. The application does not make private knowledge-base content public.

The owner controls the accounts, the workflows and whether automatic updates are activated. Permission can be revoked at any time through Google Account connections.

Information and policies

Read the Privacy Policy for details about Google user data, storage and third-party processors, or the Terms of Use for the limitations of this personal application.

For questions, use the application support email listed on its Google OAuth consent screen.
